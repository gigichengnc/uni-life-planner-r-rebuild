I took part in a weekend hackathon where our team had to design a small digital solution for a student problem. I enjoyed turning an unclear idea into a prototype, especially the stages where we compared different approaches, divided tasks, tested the interface, and explained our reasoning to judges. Some technical parts were frustrating because our first version did not work as expected, but debugging together made the experience more useful. I left the event wanting to improve my coding, data analysis, and presentation skills. The competition also showed me that innovation is not only about technology; it requires teamwork, experimentation, and the ability to learn quickly when a plan fails.
