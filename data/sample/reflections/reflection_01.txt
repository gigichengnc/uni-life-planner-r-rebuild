I joined a creative journaling and mindfulness workshop because I wanted a quiet break from a busy semester. The session combined simple drawing, breathing exercises, and a short discussion about noticing small details in daily life. I felt uncertain at first because I do not usually describe myself as artistic, but the relaxed environment made experimentation easier. By the end, I felt calmer and more curious about using creative activities to manage stress. The most valuable part was not producing a perfect piece of work; it was learning that reflection, imagination, and attention can help me understand my own emotions. I would join another workshop that combines art, culture, or mindfulness.
