I attended a student policy forum about reducing waste and improving sustainability on campus. The discussion was slower than a competition because every proposal had to consider rules, budgets, different stakeholder views, and whether the idea could actually be implemented. I liked examining evidence and asking who would be responsible for each action. At the same time, I realised that a good policy is not only about having the strongest opinion; it also requires fairness, accountability, and compromise. The forum made me more interested in student governance, environmental initiatives, and committee work where decisions have to be explained clearly and reviewed by others.
