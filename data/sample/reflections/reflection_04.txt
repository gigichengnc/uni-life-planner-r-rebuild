I joined an interfaculty debate night because I wanted to become more confident when speaking under pressure. Preparing arguments was exciting, but the live round was much harder than practice because I had to listen carefully, respond quickly, and stay composed when the other team challenged my assumptions. I was disappointed by several weak answers, yet I also enjoyed the competitive energy and the discipline required to improve. The experience encouraged me to keep training my communication, teamwork, and strategic thinking. I would be interested in future debate, public speaking, or other competitive activities that combine preparation with performance.
