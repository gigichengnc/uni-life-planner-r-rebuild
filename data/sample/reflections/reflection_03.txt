I volunteered at a community outreach day that paired university students with local families for games and learning activities. Before the event I worried that I would not know how to speak with people from very different backgrounds. Once the programme started, listening carefully became more important than trying to give advice. I enjoyed helping the group solve small problems and seeing how much trust could be built through simple conversations. The experience made me more aware of empathy, patience, and the responsibility of serving a community rather than treating volunteering as a one-day achievement. I would like to join future service projects where I can work with others over a longer period and understand their needs more deeply.
